Curriculum

5 Modules · 10 Days

Understand how large language models work at a conceptual level — tokens, context windows, temperature, and system prompts. Learn to speak the language of your engineering partners.

Design for AI agents that act autonomously on behalf of users. Learn the principles of trust, transparency, and control that govern agentic interfaces.

Build structured prompts that generate consistent UI components, design tokens, and visual assets. Create prompt libraries your team can reuse.

Hands-on with v0, Cursor, Stitch, and custom GPT workflows. Build a working prototype from scratch using AI-assisted design in under 2 hours.

Navigate the ethical dimensions of AI design: bias in training data, accessibility implications, and the evolving role of the designer in an AI-augmented product team.
