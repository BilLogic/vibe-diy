Curriculum

5 Modules.
10 Days.

Understand how large language models work at a conceptual level — tokens, context windows, temperature, and system prompts. Learn to speak the language of your engineering partners and make informed design decisions around AI capabilities and constraints.

Design for AI agents that act autonomously on behalf of users. Learn the principles of trust, transparency, and control that govern agentic interfaces — and how to prototype them effectively.

Build structured prompts that generate consistent UI components, design tokens, and visual assets. Learn to create prompt libraries that your team can reuse across projects.

Hands-on with the latest AI prototyping tools: v0, Cursor, Stitch, and custom GPT workflows. Build a working prototype from scratch using AI-assisted design in under 2 hours.

Navigate the ethical dimensions of AI design: bias in training data, accessibility implications, and the evolving role of the designer in an AI-augmented product team.
