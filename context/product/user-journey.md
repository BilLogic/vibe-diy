# User Journey

## Stage 1: Awareness

**How they find it:** Social media post, department flyer, Slack channel, word of mouth from peers

**User goal:** "What is this?"

**Key content:** Workshop title, one-line hook, visual identity

**Likely components:** Hero section with `Text` (title-hero), `Section`

## Stage 2: Consideration

**User goal:** "Is this worth my time?"

**Key content:**
- What is compound designing (2-3 sentence explanation)
- Speaker bio and credibility
- What attendees will learn (3-4 concrete outcomes)
- Format details (30 min talk + 30 min hands-on)
- Date, time, location

**Likely components:** `Section`, `Grid`, `Text`, `Avatar` (speaker photo), `Flex`

## Stage 3: Decision

**User goal:** "I want to sign up"

**Key content:** Registration CTA, any prerequisites

**Likely components:** `Button` (primary CTA), `Section`, `Text`

## Stage 4: Post-Registration

**User goal:** "What do I do next?"

**Key content:** Confirmation message, preparation instructions (install tools, create accounts), share link

**Likely components:** `Notification` (confirmation), `Text`, `Link`, `Button` (share)
