# Product Overview

## What We're Building

A **landing page** for the "Compound Designing" workshop at Carnegie Mellon University.

## Workshop Details

- **Title:** Compound Designing
- **Tagline:** How to stop repeating prompts and start building a system that gets smarter every time you design with it
- **Speaker:** Bill Guo — BHCI grad, UXA alum, Design Lead at PLUS
- **Duration:** 60 minutes (30 min talk + 30 min hands-on)
- **Audience:** CMU design students
- **Format:** In-person workshop

## Landing Page Goals

1. **Communicate value** — explain what compound designing is and why it matters
2. **Introduce the speaker** — establish credibility and approachability
3. **Drive registration** — clear CTA that makes signing up effortless
4. **Set expectations** — what attendees will learn, what they'll do, what to prepare

## Constraints

- Single page (no multi-page navigation)
- Responsive (mobile-first, works on all screen sizes)
- Accessible (WCAG 2.1 AA compliant)
- Professional but approachable tone
- Built entirely with SDS components and tokens
