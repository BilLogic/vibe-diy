# Users

## Primary Audience

**CMU design students** — undergraduate and graduate students in design programs at Carnegie Mellon University.

### Characteristics

- Mixed AI fluency: some are beginners, some have experimented with AI tools
- Design-literate: understand visual design, typography, layout principles
- Technically curious: comfortable with design tools, some exposure to code
- Time-constrained: evaluating many workshops and events competing for their attention

### Jobs to Be Done

1. **Decide if it's worth attending** — quickly understand what they'll gain
2. **Register easily** — frictionless sign-up with minimal steps
3. **Know what to prepare** — any prerequisites, tools to install, accounts to create
4. **Share with peers** — easy to forward or share on social media

## Secondary Audience

- **Faculty and advisors** evaluating workshop quality and relevance for their students
- **Design community members** who may encounter the page through social sharing

## Key Insight

These users are design students — they will judge the landing page itself as evidence of the workshop's quality. The page must demonstrate good design, not just describe it.
